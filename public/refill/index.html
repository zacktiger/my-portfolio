Can I be woken before the reset?

Yes. Open the popup, click the gear, and pick 5 or 10 minutes early — or type any number, up to two hours.

Can I use my own sound?

Behind the same gear: pick any audio file under 1.5 MB. It stays on your computer, and Test plays it so you know what you're in for.

Does it use my Claude usage?

No. It reads the limits your settings page already shows. No prompts, no API key, no account.

What if my laptop is asleep at the reset?

Chrome alarms don't fire while Chrome is closed, and fire late after sleep. An alarm hours overdue is dropped rather than rung at random. A phone ping over Telegram is next.

What does it send anywhere?

Nothing. No server, no account, no analytics. Privacy policy.
